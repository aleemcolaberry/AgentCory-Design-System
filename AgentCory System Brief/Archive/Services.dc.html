Before you write

Full-time role or project work?

Both. Companies hiring a senior or lead product designer in KSA or UAE, on-site or remote, can start with the resume. Teams with a product problem can bring me in for a scoped project, with me doing the work.

What do you need from us to start?

Access to the product, one decision-maker, and the number you want to move. Design files and research are welcome but not required; I audit what exists in the first week.

How do you hand off?

As code and spec files, not just mock-ups: tokens, components, states and a changelog your engineers, and your AI tools, can read without me in the room.
