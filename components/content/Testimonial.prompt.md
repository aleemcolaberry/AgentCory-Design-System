Customer testimonial card: leads with a gradient result metric, then quote and attribution.

```jsx
<Testimonial
  metric="847%"
  metricLabel="ROI in Year 1"
  quote="We went from 40% contact rates to 94% overnight."
  name="Dr. Sarah Johnson"
  role="Director of Admissions, Metro State University"
/>
```

Omit `photo` to render initials. Quote marks are added automatically.
