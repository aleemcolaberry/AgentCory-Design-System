Portfolio and job-search quality bar

  • Show business problem, users, product choices, trade-offs, roadmap, requirements, AI evaluation and risks.
  • Use actual metrics only when available and authorised; never invent adoption, revenue, savings or accuracy.
  • Do not disclose confidential Colaberry/client information. Use approved work or a clearly labelled independent case study.
  • Tailor CVs to product management and AI solutions; keep employment titles accurate.
  • Check vacancy requirements for Arabic, Saudi-market experience, sponsorship, relocation and location.

Saudi travel and employment checks

Visa ≠ work permit

A business/visit trip is not permission to work. Confirm the correct employment visa and work authorisation before starting.

Verify every term

Verify employer, written contract, job title, salary, housing, transport, insurance, flights, leave, probation and relocation terms.

Bilingual contract

Ensure Arabic and English contract versions agree; verify agents and visa requirements through official channels.

No ‘free visa’

Do not pay for a purported ‘free visa’ or rely on verbal assurances.
