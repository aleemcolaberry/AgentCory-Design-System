Always

  • Let sentences wrap: fluid sizes (clamp(), --text-fluid-*), text-wrap: balance on headings, pretty on body.
  • Give every flex or grid child min-width: 0; base.css does it for .cory-* layouts.
  • Shorten the copy when a line is tight. Never shrink the box.
  • Keep body lines 45 to 80 characters (.cory-prose, --measure-body).

Never

  • white-space: nowrap on anything longer than 4 words. .cory-nowrap is for numbers, codes and 1 to 2 word labels.
  • text-overflow: ellipsis or line-clamp on copy.
  • Fixed height on a box that holds a sentence.
  • Em dashes, letter-spacing on Arabic, type under 11px.
